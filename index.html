FAQ

How do I create a game?
Tap "Create Game", set a name and password, then share the code with your friends.

How do I join a game?
Tap "Join Game" and enter the game name and password shared by the host.

Is the app free?
Yes. The app is free to use. It contains ads to support development.

How do I transfer money?
On the game board, tap a player's card or use the bank buttons to send or receive money.
